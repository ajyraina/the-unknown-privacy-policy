Privacy Policy

` `

Last updated: October 17, 2026

` `

The Unknown is a personal content-publishing application used to prepare and publish posts to a Facebook Page.

` `

Information We Process

` `

The application may process Facebook Page information and publishing permissions needed to create and publish posts. It may also process post text, images, and book links supplied by the operator.

` `

How Information Is Used

` `

Information is used to operate the publishing workflow, prepare content, and publish posts to the Facebook Page selected by the operator.

` `

Sharing

` `

Content submitted for publication is sent to Facebook as directed by the operator. We do not sell personal information.

Publishing records may remain in the application for operational purposes. To request deletion of information associated with this application, contact the email address below.
